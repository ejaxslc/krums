Our story

A healthier, crispier way to snack.

Krums started in 2026 with one simple idea: cookies deserved a thinner, crispier and healthier way to snack. We make cookie brittle with thoughtfully chosen ingredients that taste exactly like a normal cookie with enough crunch to leave a trail.
