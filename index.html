Questions?We’ve got krums.

What exactly are Krums?+

Krums is cookie brittle: a thinner, crispier cookie that tastes like the one you already love, with enough crunch to leave a trail.

How much sugar is in a serving?+

Each serving has 3g of sugar and 130 calories, with 50% less sugar than a typical cookie.

What does 4g net carbs mean?+

4g net carbs = 17g carbs − 1g fiber − 11g allulose. FDA does not consider allulose a total or added sugar for nutrition labeling.
